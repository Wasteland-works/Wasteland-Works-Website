Game studio

We develop original games, playable prototypes, world-building tools, characters and interactive systems. Our current flagship project is the post-apocalyptic RPG Cascade Failure.

Hardware modification

We rebuild, modify and reinterpret real hardware—from handheld computers and wearable interfaces to experimental controllers, displays and one-off workshop machines.

PIP Core

PIP Core is our custom game engine and authoring technology. It grew from a bare-metal Mac prototype into the technical foundation powering Cascade Failure.
